Teaching/Mechatronics simulators
EME154 · Interactive Learning

Explore the mechatronics simulators.

Choose a lab to investigate a system, change its settings, and follow what happens.

The labs run in your browser. Start with a guided example, then use the controls to test your own ideas. Each page explains the assumptions behind its model.

Microcontrollers and timing

AVR architectureAVR Architecture ExplorerTrace buses, memory, registers, the ALU, status flags, and the stack. AVR assemblyAVR CPU & Assembly LabWrite a short program and step through its effect on registers and SRAM. TimersTimer LabExplore prescalers, counter ticks, overflow, and compare events. InterruptsTimer Interrupt LabFollow a Timer1 compare match through an ISR and back to the main program.

Motion and measurement

EncodersEncoder & Speed LabConnect quadrature signals to position counts and speed estimates. Position samplingPosition Counter & Sampling LabSee how wraparound and sample timing affect reconstructed motion. Frequency responseFrequency Response LabLink Bode plots to the motion of open and closed loop systems.

Control and drives

Position controlPosition Control LabCompare feedback, feedforward, following error, and in-position behavior. Motor drivesMotor & Drive LabInvestigate H-bridges, PWM, power conversion, flyback, and three-phase fields.

Companion calculators

Timer setupTimer CalculatorCalculate ATmega328P CTC settings and inspect the generated Arduino code. Measurement errorSpeed Measurement Error CalculatorCompare pulse counting and pulse duration across a speed range.
